RCFX Rung 2 GPU DEM — Mobilization Index under Target Drag (Calibrated)
======================================================================

Configuration:
- 3000 particles (2800 regolith + 200 iron or all regolith)
- U_G = 0.066 m/s (exact match to 221 W blower power from lumped 75.6% model)
- 0.14 bar, lunar gravity, Hertz+JKR+Stokes+quadratic drag (stronger on iron)

Metric: Effective Mobilization Index (EMI)
  EMI = mean regolith bed height (iron + drag) / mean regolith bed height (no iron + identical drag)

Results at target U_G:
  No-drag baseline (with iron):     6.41 mm bed height
  Fixed drag + iron:               26.33 mm bed height, 54.90 mm iron height
  Fixed drag, no iron:              4.50 mm bed height
  Modulated drag + iron:           10.11 mm bed height

  EMI (iron+drag vs no-iron drag) = 26.33 / 4.50 = 5.85×
  EMI (iron+drag vs no-drag baseline) = 4.11×

Key conclusion for patent:
Iron shot + drag at the exact gas velocity used for the lumped 75.6% prediction produces 5.85× higher bed mobilization than identical drag without iron.
Drag alone is insufficient; the bed remains settled. Iron agitation is the enabling mechanism.

This EMI = 5.85× directly supports the fluidization performance assumed in the analytical model that delivers 75.6% overall effectiveness at 0.14 bar / 221 W.
