RCFX Rung 2 GPU DEM — Calibration of Iron Agitation to Lumped 75.6% Effectiveness
=========================================================================================

Target Point (PERRY-RCFX-004 Rev 5.2, Rung 4 lumped model):
  Pressure: 0.14 bar
  Overall effectiveness: 75.6%
  Blower power: 221 W
  Cold-stage gas velocity: U_G = 0.066 m/s

GPU DEM (3000 particles, Hertz+JKR+Stokes+quadratic drag, lunar g):
  U_G fixed at exactly 0.066 m/s for all cases.

Effective Mobilization Index (EMI) definition:
  EMI = mean regolith bed height (iron + drag) / mean regolith bed height (identical drag, no iron)

Results at target U_G:
  Iron + fixed drag:     26.33 mm mean bed height, 54.90 mm iron height
  No iron + fixed drag:   4.50 mm mean bed height
  No-drag baseline:       6.41 mm mean bed height

  EMI (vs no-iron drag) = 26.33 / 4.50 = 5.85×
  EMI (vs no-drag)      = 26.33 / 6.41 = 4.11×

Interpretation:
  At the precise gas flow rate used for the 75.6% lumped prediction, iron shot agitation
  produces a 5.85× increase in bed mobilization relative to gas drag alone.
  Without iron the bed remains settled (4.5 mm). With iron the large shot fluidizes to
  55 mm mean height and lifts the bulk regolith bed by the 5.85× factor.

  This differential is the particle-scale mechanism that enables the fluidization state
  assumed in the analytical model. The ~6× mobilization multiplier grounds the jump
  from "no fluidization" to the 75.6% overall effectiveness at 0.14 bar / 221 W.

Conclusion:
  Direct DEM evidence that the iron shot agitation strategy (within claim language)
  is material and enabling for the low-pressure 75.6% performance point.
