RCFX utility support bundle (2026-06-05)

Five-stage counter-current low-pressure fluidized bed with dual-role iron shot
(PERRY-RCFX-004 Rev 5.2). Modeling only; no hardware prototype.

- Lumped 5-stage model: 75.6% effectiveness, 221 W blower (1.88% parasitic) at 0.14 bar.
- Custom GPU DEM Rung 1: high-N physical-lid series plus good-variable real-drag
  point (1.5 mm iron, 3.5 m/s, EMI 3.58×, 100% inside lid).
- Envelope for claims: 1.5–2.0 mm iron, ~2.5–3.5+ m/s cold stages, 0.1–0.5 bar.

Any physical plant still needs conventional vessel seals and feed/discharge
interfaces. Those are not demonstrated here.
