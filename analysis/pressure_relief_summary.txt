RCFX Pressure Relief Levers — First Quantitative Results
Date: 31 May 2026
Model: pressure_relief_levers_v1.py (anchored to Rev 5.2 parameters)

GOAL
Find the lowest envelope pressure at which we can still get acceptable performance using only the mitigations the current architecture already contains:
- Iron shot agitation (mechanical agglomerate breaking + thermal mass)
- EDS charge dissipation
- Pre-classification / fines management

KEY RESULT (simplified but directionally robust model)

At 0.10 bar:
- Even with maximum iron agitation (0.9) + best EDS (0.95) + aggressive pre-classification (25 µm cutoff):
  → Overall effectiveness ~0.45–0.50
  This is well below the 70% floor. The cold-stage fines problem wins.

At 0.12 bar:
- Max iron + max EDS + no pre-class: ~0.57 overall
- Still marginal for the design target.

At 0.15 bar:
- High iron (0.9) + high EDS (0.95) + no pre-classification: ~0.65 overall
- Getting close to usable.

At 0.20 bar (current nominal target):
- High iron + high EDS + no pre-class: ~0.77 overall
- Matches the "design target 80-90%" range in the spec (this model is conservative).

CONCLUSIONS (preliminary)

1. The existing three mitigations in the patent (iron shot tumbling, EDS, and fines management) can realistically support operation down to approximately **0.14–0.16 bar** before effectiveness drops below ~65-70%.

2. Below ~0.12 bar, the cohesive fines in the cold stages (lightest gas + first contact with raw bimodal regolith) cause catastrophic loss of performance even when we max out iron agitation and EDS. The physics is brutal at those densities.

3. The "free" lever the system already has — routing heavier CO/CO2 gases preferentially to the colder stages — is extremely valuable and should be engineered for (not left to chance).

4. To push reliably into the 0.08–0.12 bar regime while keeping effectiveness ≥75%, we will almost certainly need one or more additional low-pressure-friendly features beyond the current architecture, for example:
   - Mild external vibration or pulsed gas assist on the early stages
   - More aggressive / optimized iron shot size distribution for agitation (smaller shot in cold stages?)
   - Better gas composition management / recirculation to deliver heavier molecules earlier
   - Different bed geometry or internal baffles to improve mixing at low density
